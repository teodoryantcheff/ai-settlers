# Keep play map-led and provide dedicated analysis views

Superseded on 2026-10-15 by [ADR-0004](0004-full-map-and-game-windows.md): preserve live analysis and its information, but replace reserved panel space and dedicated workspaces with a full-viewport map and temporary game windows. The decision below records the earlier design.

The interface must support both spatial settlement building and detailed economic diagnosis. Keep the map as the primary workspace with persistent colony summaries and a docked inspector, and give Economy and Logistics dedicated analytical views rather than making tables the default workspace or requiring overlapping floating panels. Accepted on 2026-10-14, this preserves observing and building the settlement as the central experience while allowing deeper analysis enough space and navigation back to affected places.
