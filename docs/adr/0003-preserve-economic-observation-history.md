# Preserve bounded economic history and colony lifetime totals

Economic analysis needs trustworthy recent trends and continuity when buildings disappear or colonies are saved. Retain ten simulation minutes of recent chart history and colony lifetime totals across save/load, preserving lifetime colony totals after demolition instead of reconstructing them only from surviving buildings. Accepted on 2026-10-14, this requires durable observational measurements with explicit coverage for older saves; paused or offline time must not manufacture observations or advance the simulation.
